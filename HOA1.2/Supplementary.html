Kimchi-Jjigae: A Spicy Korean Delight

Kimchi stew is one of the most-loved of all the stews in Korean cuisine. It’s a warm, hearty, spicy, savory, delicious dish that pretty much everyone loves. As long as they can handle spicy food, I never met a person who didn’t like kimchi-jjigae.

Ingredients:

  • Kimchi: 1 Pound
  • Pork Belly: 1/2 pounds
  • Tofu: sliced into ½ inch thick bite size pieces (Optional)
  • Medium Onion: Sliced 1 cup
  • Spicy Red Korean Paste: 4 Table Spoon
  • Sesame Oil: 2 teaspoons
  • Enoki Mushroom: 1 Pack (Optional)

Preparation:

  1. Put the anchovies, daikon, green onion roots, and dried kelp in a sauce pan..
  2. Add the water and boil for 20 minutes over medium high heat.
  3. Lower the heat to low for another 5 minutes.
  4. Strain
  5. Place the kimchi and kimchi brine in a shallow pot. Add pork and onion
  6. Slice 2 green onions diagonally and add them to the pot.
  7. Add salt, sugar, hot pepper flakes, and hot pepper paste. Drizzle sesame oil over top and add the anchovy stock
  9. Cover and cook for 10 minutes over medium high heat.
  10. Open and mix in the seasonings with a spoon. Lay the tofu over top.
  11. Cover and cook another 10 to 15 minutes over medium heat.
  12. Chop 1 green onion and put it on the top of the stew. Remove from the heat and serve right away with rice.

Cooking Tips:

  • 1/4 Use Aged Kimchi: The single most important tip is to use older, well-fermented (even slightly overripe) kimchi. This sourness is crucial for the deep, complex flavor of the stew; fresh kimchi will result in a bland, spicy cabbage soup. If your kimchi isn't sour enough, you can leave it at room temperature for a day or two to ferment further
  • 1 Simmer Low and Slow: Don't rush the process. After bringing the stew to a boil, reduce the heat and let it simmer for at least 20-30 minutes so all the ingredients can meld together and the kimchi becomes tender.
